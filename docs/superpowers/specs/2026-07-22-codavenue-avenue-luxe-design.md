# CODAvenue Avenue Luxe Design Spec

## Goal

Make CODAvenue feel more premium while preserving the existing Investir Alia-inspired cream, ink navy, and champagne color direction.

## Direction

The approved direction is Avenue Luxe: editorial commerce, refined spacing, thinner lines, subtle champagne accents, dark ink contrast, elevated product imagery, and a custom CA monogram logo.

## Scope

- Add a reusable code-native logo component with a CA avenue/gateway monogram and CODAvenue wordmark.
- Refine global tokens, buttons, fields, and surface treatments.
- Upgrade the header, footer, homepage, category grid, product cards, catalogue shell, and admin order page.
- Keep the React/Vite/Tailwind stack and current routing/API behavior unchanged.

## Constraints

- Keep the design bilingual-ready and avoid hardcoded behavior changes.
- Use SVG/HTML/CSS for the logo so it scales cleanly.
- Do not add new runtime dependencies.
- Keep card radii at 8px or less.
